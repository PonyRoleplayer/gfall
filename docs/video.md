# Movies (VID)

`ANIM0000..0015.VID` and `DAG2.VID` in ARENA2 are the game's movies: 320x200 (DAG2 256x200), 8 bits under their own palette, a delta/RLE codec, 8-bit unsigned mono sound at about 11 kHz, 60 Hz frame ticks. 17 files, about 100 MB. The princes' `*.FLC` (Autodesk FLIC, 16 files, 320x200, 14 frames, 600 to 700 KB each) are separate (below).

## The format (`src/game/dagger/formats/vid.h`)
FALL.EXE's player is `xn_vid_play` (decomp `src/engine/vid.c`, `xvid.h`). A 15-byte header (`VID\0`, version, frames, width, height, per-frame delay, flags), then chunks by type byte: 0 raw, 1 delta, 4 delta from a start row, 3 RLE (each with a word delay in ticks), 2 palette (768 bytes, six bits a channel), 7Ch audio header (delay, rate byte: rate = 1000000 / (256 - byte)), 7Dh audio data (word length, bytes). A frame is followed at once by its audio. `vid_next` decodes up to the next frame; sound goes to a callback.

Every movie decodes to its last frame in hostcheck (`check_vid`), with the original's frame counts; frames of 0, 12 and DAG2 are written to `build/vid*.ppm`. Cost on the 233: a read window of 128 KB and a 64000-byte frame, kept for the process; decoding is copies and fills.

## Where they play (decomp)
- `ANIM0012`: death. `ANIM0002` and `ANIM0004`: the lycanthropy and vampirism dreams on the first rest. `ANIM0013`: the ghost's farewell (quest op 50 of `S0000015`). Quest op 50 plays `anim%04d.vid`. The intro and Bethesda logo (`intro.c`). The princes' FLC in the summoning.

## Where they are read from
GFALL assumes a full ("huge") installation: the whole of ARENA2, movies included, on the hard disk. `fs_open_media` (engine/platform/fs.c) opens a movie from the game root first, and only when the install lacks it tries the CD-ROM drives (`X:\\DAGGER\\ARENA2`, then `X:\\ARENA2`: the original disc), by `GetDriveTypeA`. No disc and no file: the movie is skipped. Host tests give the "disc" through `GFALL_MEDIA`. The movies are the only files that fall back to the CD.

## Playing them (`src/game/dagger/play/ui/movie.c`)
`movie_play(n)` / `movie_play_file(name)` queue a movie; `play_frame` calls `movie_frame(dt, skip)` while `movie_active()` (nothing else moves) and `play_draw` calls `movie_draw()`. The first movie of a run puts the music aside (`music_set_on(0)`, `sound_stop_all()`) and the last gives it back. A frame is one 320 x 200 P_8 picture (`pic_load`, then `pic_update` per frame: only the tiles that changed go to the card, 29 tiles, about 66 KB of the pictures' room (`pic_set_room`)); a palette chunk is `pic_set_palette` (a movie has one, at the start; docs/pictures.md, A new palette). The sound is `sound_stream_open(rate)` / `sound_stream_push` as the reader finds each chunk (docs/sound.md, A stream); the frame clock is the wall clock, set to the sound's (`sound_stream_queued`, the samples not yet mixed) when they drift 100 ms apart, and with no card (open gives 0, queued -1) the ticks alone pace it. Up to 8 frames are decoded in a call when behind, drawn once; a longer stall drops the lost time. Escape, Enter, Space or a click skips the movie that plays (the next queued one follows); a missing movie is passed over. DAG2 (256 wide) is centred (x 32).
Where: the logo (ANIM0001) and the opening (ANIM0000, ANIM0011, DAG2) at start (`gfall_play_startup`), the funeral (ANIM0012) once per death, the dreams (`rest_set_dream`, `DREAM_MOVIE_*`) and quest op 50 (`QOP_PLAY_MOVIE`). `movie_set_enabled(0)` turns them off (the host's default, so the tests' title frames are theirs; `check_movie` turns them on). `skip_opening=on` in `gfall/settings.cfg` (`CONFIG_FLAG_SKIP_OPENING`) leaves out the logo and the three opening movies. While a movie or a scene plays `seam_screen_covers` is true, so `main.c` draws no world behind it (`gfx_frame(&cam, 0)`): DAG2's side bars and the letterbox are black.
## The princes' FLC (`formats/flc.c`, `play/ui/movie.c`)
`AZURA.FLC` .. `VAERNIMA.FLC`: Autodesk FLC, a 128-byte header (size, magic AF12h, 14 frames, 320 x 200, 8 bits, speed 214 ms, 165 ms for Peryite, 110 for Vaernima, the first and second frames' offsets at 80 and 84), a prefix chunk, then frames of F1FAh, each a few chunks: 18 (thumbnail, skipped), 4 (palette, **8 bits** a channel: the values go to 255, not the DAC's six), 15 BRUN (the first frame), 7 SS2 (word deltas, the other 14); 11 (six-bit palette), 13, 16 and 12 (LC) are read too though no file has them. FALL.EXE's `flc_open`, `flc_read_frame`, `flc_decode_brun` and `flc_decode_ss2` (decomp `pflc.c`, `func_00052B50.c`) are the model. The last frame is a delta back to the first, so a loop is the second frame on, 14 frames again (`flc_rewind`, as `flc_play_with_text` does), and one frame lasts `speed / 55 + 1` BIOS ticks of 55 ms (220 ms for 214). `check_flc` plays each file through and requires the last frame to leave the first frame's pixels exactly (a strong check of the SS2 decoder), 14 frames in the loop, and a first frame with a palette; it writes `build/flc0.ppm`.
`movie_scene(file, words, ask, done)` is `flc_play_with_text`: the file loops over the screen (one P_8 picture, `pic_update` per frame, the palette its own) with a page of the words in four lines from y 150, ten apart, centred on x 160, drawn into the frame with FONT0003 in palette index 145 (the FLC's yellow) with 156 (brown) as the shadow. A click, Enter or Space turns the page; on the last a scene that asks nothing leaves (answer 0), and one that asks waits for Y (1) or N (2): the original has no other key, we also take Escape as a no. It is used for the summoning's second success (TEXT.RSC 482, `%dae` the prince, `%dat` the date, in `Words.daedra` and `.date`; the servant is put beside the player when it ends) and for a prince's quest offer: `quest_begin` shows message 1000 in his scene, asking, then 1002 or 1001 in it again without a question (`quest_set_scene`; the quest file's first letter picks the file through `prince_flc`, `g_prince_flc` the pointer table at 0x1850E5 in the order of `TUVWXYZ123456789`). With movies off, no file or no card the same words come in the message box as before. A scene may be started from its own answer; turning movies off drops it without calling back. `check_scene` and `check_quest`'s prince offers test it with a stub scene.

Video through a REALmagic card is not planned: the files are not MPEG, and a Voodoo 1's pass-through owns the monitor in a game.
